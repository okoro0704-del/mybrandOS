# Public Experience + Trust Boundary V1

**Consumption does not require identity. Authority does.**

Public mybrandOS App and Space are `GUEST`-safe: public feeds, media, TV, Radio, News and Digipedia require no TrustID or passkey. `IDENTIFIED` is a TrustID-backed product session. `STEP_UP_VERIFIED` is reserved for sensitive policy-controlled actions; it is not a consumer entrance requirement.

Studio remains TrustID, relationship, and server-capability authorized. A public URL, installed Space, local browser state, or creator slug never confers Studio authority. Presentation (`APP`/`SPACE`) and trust are independent dimensions.
