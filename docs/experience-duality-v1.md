# Experience Duality V1

Every product has an explicit presentation mode: `APP` or `SPACE`. App Experience is the conventional UI-first product and owns its normal routes, navigation, forms and media controls. Space Experience is opt-in, Space Contract-compatible, and owns Glass, the reveal handle, Switch, Revolve and Space capability mediation.

Installing/opening an ordinary web app in OS Xperience does not grant the Space Stack. Product state remains app-owned; Space snapshots stay namespaced by `space-contract-v1:<spaceId>`. Authentication remains the same product/TrustID session. TV, radio and calls are Space capability directions, with product programming/branding; extraction is out of scope.
